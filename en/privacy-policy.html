Privacy Policy

iPhone app "Switch Express" (切り替え特急)

This policy explains how the iPhone app "Switch Express" (the "App") handles information. The Japanese version is the authoritative one.

1. Information the App itself collects

The App has no accounts or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number or other personal information. Your coins, locomotives, cargo, records, missions and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. Deleting the App deletes them.

2. Information sent for advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may send Google device identifiers (the IDFA only if you allow tracking), approximate location derived from your IP address, information about ads shown and your interactions with them, app usage, device type, OS version and crash information. We do not receive this information; we only see aggregated figures such as impressions and revenue. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Tracking permission

On first launch, the App asks through Apple's App Tracking Transparency whether you allow tracking with the IDFA. Every feature works the same if you decline; ads just won't be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Coins and purchases

Coins are earned in the game (picking them up while running, completing missions). They cannot be bought or exchanged for money. The App has no in-app purchases.

5. Sharing your record

The share feature only hands an image and text of your record to the app you choose through iOS sharing. We never receive it.

6. Children

The App is suitable for a wide audience but is not made specifically for children. We collect no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page. Contact: penguinnonawabari@gmail.com
